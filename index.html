One brain for every power source.

Smart Power Brain sits between your electrical panel and your grid, solar, battery and generator. It helps protect your appliances from damaging power, and switches to the healthiest source for you.

Bad power does more damage than blackouts

Voltage swings, surges and floating neutral faults quietly wear out fridges, TVs and batteries. Stabilizers, surge protectors and transfer switches each cover one part of the problem, and none of them talk to each other.

Surges and spikes

Lightning and grid switching push brief, violent voltage into your sockets.

Sags and overvoltage

Sustained low or high voltage overheats motors and compressors.

Floating neutral

A broken neutral can send dangerously high voltage to some outlets while others go dim.

Two layers of protection

  1. Clamp the instant events

    A passive protection stage limits fast surges before they reach your panel.

  2. Watch everything else

    SPB measures voltage, current, frequency and neutral health all the time.

  3. Switch to a safer source

    When the supply turns unsafe, SPB isolates it and moves compatible loads to a healthier source. Loads that can't be powered safely are disconnected.

SPB reduces your exposure to damaging power. It does not make appliances immune, and good earthing is still required.
